## 🇹🇷 Türkçe

Chest Tracker, Fortnite oynarken açtığın sandıkları arka planda otomatik olarak sayar. Hiçbir ayar, hiçbir lisans anahtarı, hiçbir uğraş yok — kur, oyna, say.

### Nasıl çalışır?

Fortnite her maçında bilgisayarına bir **replay (tekrar) dosyası** kaydeder. Chest Tracker bu dosyaları okuyup o maçta açtığın sandıkları çıkarır ve toplamına ekler. Yani oyunu hiçbir şekilde etkilemez, oyunun içine girmez, hafızasına dokunmaz — sadece Fortnite'ın kendi kaydettiği tekrar dosyalarını okur.

### Kurulum

1. Yukarıdaki **İNDİR** düğmesine bas ve en son sürümün `ChestTracker-Setup-...exe` dosyasını indir.
2. Çalıştır. Tek tıkla kurulur, kendisi açılır. Hepsi bu.
3. Fortnite'ı aç ve oyna. Sandıkların sayılmaya başlar.

> **İlk açılışta son 100 maçın yüklenir.** Fortnite bilgisayarında yalnızca **son ~100 replay dosyasını** saklar. Chest Tracker ilk kez açıldığında bu mevcut replay'leri tarar; böylece sıfırdan değil, **son 100 maçının** sandıklarıyla başlarsın. O andan sonra oynadığın her yeni maç otomatik olarak üstüne eklenir. (Fortnite daha eski replay'leri zaten silmiş olduğu için 100'den öncesi okunamaz — bu Fortnite'ın sınırı, uygulamanın değil.)

### ⚠️ Bilmen gereken iki şey

- **Sandıkların sayılması için programın çalışıyor olması gerekir.** Program kapalıyken açtığın sandıklar o anda okunamaz. Ama merak etme — program Windows ile birlikte **kendi kendine arka planda başlar** (aşağıya bak), yani normalde hep çalışır. Sadece Görev Yöneticisi'nden elle kapatma.
- **Öldükten sonra açtığın sandıklar sayılmaz.** Fortnite'ın replay dosyası yalnızca sen hayattayken çevreni kaydeder. Elendikten sonra açtığın/gördüğün sandıklar tekrar dosyasına düzgün geçmediğinden sayıma giremez. Bu yüzden maç sonu toplamın, oyunda gördüğün sayıdan bazen biraz düşük olabilir.

- Program Windows açıldığında **kendi kendine arka planda başlar** — ekrana bir pencere **gelmez**, tepsi simgesi bile yoktur. Sayacı görmek istediğinde masaüstü/başlat menüsündeki **Chest Tracker** kısayoluna tıklaman yeterli.

## 🗝️ Chest Tracker (English)

**A desktop app that counts the chests you open in Fortnite — automatically, permanently, and effortlessly.**

[**⬇️ DOWNLOAD (Windows)**](../../releases/latest) · [All releases](../../releases)

</div>

Chest Tracker counts the chests you open while playing Fortnite, quietly in the background. No settings, no license key, no hassle — install it, play, and it counts.

### How it works

Fortnite saves a **replay file** on your PC for every match you play. Chest Tracker reads those files, extracts the chests you opened that match, and adds them to your total. It never touches the game itself — it doesn't inject into Fortnite or read its memory; it only reads the replay files Fortnite already writes.

### Installation

1. Click **DOWNLOAD** above and grab the latest `ChestTracker-Setup-...exe`.
2. Run it. It installs in one click and launches itself. That's it.
3. Open Fortnite and play. Your chests start counting.

> **On first launch, your last 100 matches are loaded.** Fortnite keeps only your **last ~100 replay files** on your PC. The first time Chest Tracker runs, it scans those existing replays, so you start with the chests from your **last 100 matches** rather than from zero. From then on, every new match you play is added automatically. (Anything older than those 100 replays is gone because Fortnite already deleted it — that's Fortnite's limit, not the app's.)

### ⚠️ Two things to know

- **The program must be running for your chests to be counted.** Chests you open while it's closed aren't read at that moment. But don't worry — the program **starts by itself in the background** with Windows (see below), so it's normally always running. Just don't kill it from Task Manager.
- **Chests you open after you die aren't counted.** Fortnite's replay only records your surroundings while you're alive. Chests you open or see after being eliminated don't make it into the replay cleanly, so they can't be counted. That's why your end-of-match total can sometimes be slightly lower than what you saw in-game.

### Highlights

- **🔢 Automatic, permanent counting** — Chests in every match are counted automatically and your total is saved permanently.
- **🔑 No key, no setup** — You never enter a license key. The app recognizes you automatically from your Fortnite/Epic account.
- **☁️ Your totals never disappear** — Your counts are tied to your Epic account. Even if Fortnite deletes old replays, you reinstall the app, or you **wipe your PC**, your counter isn't reset — reinstall and it picks up right where you left off.
- **👥 Multiple accounts** — If you play with more than one Epic account on the same PC, each account's count is tracked **separately**. The app detects the account switch on its own.
- **🍀 Luck meter** — Your chance at rare rewards is estimated from how many chests you've opened, and rises as you open more.
- **🎁 Reward tracking** — Notable rewards from a match are listed alongside the chests.
- **🎉 Live celebration alerts** — When someone in the community finds a special reward (a Token), a 15-second celebration (confetti + sound) pops up in the bottom-right of your screen. It appears **on top of** the game but **doesn't steal focus and is click-through** — if you click it, the click goes to the game behind it, so your game is never interrupted. It fades out on its own after 15 seconds. Announcements work the same way.
- **🛒 Fortnite item shop** — Today's shop, inside the app. Details below.
- **🛠️ Report an issue (tickets)** — Hit a bug or have a request? You can **report it right from inside the app**. Your report reaches the admin as a tracked ticket.
- **🌍 Languages** — English is the main language, with Turkish and many other common languages built in. The app picks your system language automatically, and you can switch it from the menu.
- **📊 Stats & charts** — Per-match breakdowns, charts, an include/exclude-team-chests toggle, plus always-on-top and compact views.

### 🛒 Fortnite item shop (new in 1.0.9)

You don't need to launch the game or check another site to see today's Fortnite item shop — click the **bag icon** in the title bar and the shop opens right inside the app.

- **The whole daily shop** — outfits, back blings, pickaxes, gliders, emotes, cars and jam tracks, organised **section by section** just like in-game.
- **Prices and discounts** — every item's V-Bucks price; if it's on sale, the old price is struck through next to the new one.
- **New items flagged** — anything entering the shop that day carries a **NEW** badge.
- **Bundles** — a card shows how many extra pieces a bundle contains (e.g. **+3**).
- **Rarity colours** — the colour strip under each card shows the item's rarity.
- **Jam tracks** — the artist is shown under the track title.
- **⏱️ Exactly how long until it refreshes — to the second.** A **live countdown** runs at the top of the shop, along with the **exact local time** the refresh happens in your own time zone (the shop rotates worldwide at 00:00 UTC).
- **Refreshes by itself** — the moment the countdown hits zero the list updates automatically; no restart, no button to press.
- **In your language** — item names arrive in the same language as the app. Change the language in the menu and the shop follows.
- **Keeps working if the source goes down** — if the service the shop data comes from has an outage, the app switches to a backup source (item names then show in English, and the source is credited on screen). If that fails too, the last saved list is shown with a clear warning on top — stale data is never passed off as current.

### 🔄 Runs in the background, uses no resources

- The program **starts by itself in the background** when Windows boots — **no window pops up** on your screen, there isn't even a tray icon. Whenever you want to see your counter, just click the **Chest Tracker** shortcut on your desktop / Start menu.
- It uses **almost no resources.** All it does is read a replay file whenever Fortnite writes a new one. It doesn't affect game performance; CPU and RAM usage are negligible.

### ⬆️ Automatic updates

The app updates itself **automatically and silently** — you never have to do anything. When a new version is released, it's downloaded in the background, verified (SHA-256), and installed. **Most importantly:** when an update happens, **no "updated" window suddenly pops onto your screen**; the app simply keeps running quietly in the background afterward. Your totals and settings are preserved across updates.
